# How to Configure Notifications for Slack (Pro)

This feature requires the Pro version of the PublishPress Planner plugin.

The Pro version of [PublishPress Planner](https://publishpress.com/links/planner) allows you to send notifications about content changes via Slack.

- These settings are available from Planner > Settings > Slack.
- Click the button “Click to connect to Slack”.

![Slack Connect Start](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-planner/slack-connect-start-e1949868d2.webp)

*Slack Connect Start*

- You will be redirected here to PublishPress.com.
- Click the “Add to Slack” button.

![Slack Step 1](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-planner/slack-step-1-2807d4844d.webp)

*Slack Step 1*

- You will now be redirected to Slack.com.
- Log in to your Slack account.
- Click “Allow”.

![Slack Step 2](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-planner/slack-step-2-4a56d2257e.webp)

*Slack Step 2*

- You will now be given an authentication code. Make sure to copy this code.

![Slack Step 3](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-planner/slack-step-3-2d10f0cccc.webp)

*Slack Step 3*

- Go back to your WordPress site.
- Paste your new code into your site’s settings.
- Click “Continue”.

![Slack Enter Code](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-planner/slack-enter-code-b9b96fd6b6.webp)

*Slack Enter Code*

- You will see a green success message if the process has worked correctly.

![Slack Congratulations](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-planner/slack-congratulations-eb19bea928.webp)

*Slack Congratulations*

- Finally, you can test the connection to Slack. Click the button in the image below to test your new connection:

![6 Success](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-planner/6-success-855132c066.webp)

*6 Success*

- You will see a test message in the Slack channel that you have chosen:
