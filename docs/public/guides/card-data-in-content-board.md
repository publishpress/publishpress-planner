The [Content Board](https://publishpress.com/links/content-board) Screen is available in the [PublishPress Planner](https://publishpress.com/links/planner?swcfpc=1) plugin. This screen gives you an overview of the content on your site. Each post is shown as a “card” and you can choose the data to see on those card.

## Introducing Content Board Cards [#](#0-toc-title)

There are four default data items on each card: Post Date, Title, Post Type, and Author. You can see those data items in this screenshot:

![Data on Content Board cards](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-planner/content-board-cards-f1a16cbc7b.webp)

![Customize Card Data](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-planner/customize-card-7b0f4a9791.webp)

In the modal, you can check or uncheck the boxes for all the inbuilt columns.

![Customize Card data](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-planner/customize-card-data-00bac20b5b.webp)

In this screenshot below, you can see columns for Editorial Fields and Taxonomies. If you click any of these elements, they will be added as new columns on your [Content Overview](https://publishpress.com/links/content-overview) screen.

![Card data taxonomies](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-planner/taxonomies-card-data-ab1ade406d.webp)

This next screenshot shows the cards with the extra “Categories” data item.

## Add Custom Metadata to Cards [#](#3-toc-title)

The [Content Board](https://publishpress.com/links/content-board) screen allows you to add custom data. This data can use any metadata stored on your site by WordPress plugins.

- Click the “Customize Card Data” button.
- Click the “Add New” button.

![](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-planner/regular-price-woo-1-f8145c7577.webp)

- Enter a “Column Title”.
- Click the “Select Metakey” option.
- In this screenshot below, we are selecting the “_regularprice” option. This will show the price of a WooCommerce product..
- Click “Add Column”.
- Click “Apply Changes”.

You will now see the “Regular Price” data has been added to your card data.

## How to Reorder Card Data [#](#4-toc-title)

It is possible to change the order of card data in the Content Board screen

- Click “Customize Card Data”.
- Click the “Reorder” tab.
- You can drag-and-drop the data into a new order.

![Reorder card data](https://docs-media.publishpress.com/publishpress-planner/reorder-card-data-32a981ffdf.webp)
